Desert-inspired ingredients

Thoughtful formulas, rooted in the Southwest.

Sunbleach draws inspiration from ingredients associated with the region and pairs them with familiar skincare staples. The fictional formulas are imagined as vegan and cruelty-free, with a focus on hydration, comfort, and daily sun protection.

Prickly pear Jojoba oil Blue agave Glycerin Ceramides Zinc oxide

Made for a dry-climate routine

Hydrate: humectants such as glycerin help attract water to the skin.

Support: emollients and barrier-supporting ingredients help soften skin and reduce moisture loss.

Protect: broad-spectrum sunscreen helps protect skin from UVA and UVB exposure when used as directed.

Your routine starting point

For dry or sensitive skin, start with a gentle cleanser, apply a moisturizer while skin is slightly damp, and finish with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. For oily or combination skin, use a lighter layer of moisturizer rather than skipping it. If you spend time outside, reapply sunscreen at least every two hours and after swimming or sweating, following the label directions.
