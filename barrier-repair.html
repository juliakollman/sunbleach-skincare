KEEP IT GENTLE
The basics

What is your skin barrier?

Your skin barrier is the outermost layer of your skin. It helps limit water loss and protects against irritants from the environment. When it is disrupted, skin may feel dry, rough, tight, or more sensitive than usual.

Signs your skin needs a break

  • Dryness or tightness, especially after cleansing.
  • Flaking or rough patches that are new for you.
  • Stinging or burning when applying products that usually feel comfortable.
  • Redness or sensitivity that seems worse after using several active products.

These signs can have different causes, so they do not confirm a damaged skin barrier on their own.

What can disrupt the barrier?

  • Frequent washing with harsh cleansers or very hot water.
  • Overusing exfoliating acids, scrubs, or retinoid products.
  • Dry environmental conditions and low humidity.
  • Using products that irritate your skin or do not suit your skin type.

In a desert climate, dry air and long periods in air conditioning can make moisture loss especially noticeable.

Back to the basics

Three habits that support your skin.

You do not need an elaborate routine to care for your skin barrier. Start with gentle cleansing, regular moisturizing, and daily sun protection.

Keep cleansing gentle

Choose a mild cleanser, use lukewarm water, and avoid scrubbing. Wash according to your skin's needs rather than repeatedly trying to remove every trace of oil.

Moisturize consistently

Look for a moisturizer with ingredients such as glycerin, ceramides, or suitable emollients. Apply after washing to help reduce water loss and improve comfort.

Pause irritating extras

If your skin is stinging or peeling, consider temporarily pausing exfoliants and other nonessential active products until it feels comfortable again.

Your reset routine

A simple morning and evening plan.

Give your routine time to work. Introduce new products one at a time, and stop using anything that causes persistent irritation.

MORNING

Cleanse if needed

Use a gentle cleanser or rinse with lukewarm water if that works for your skin. Apply a moisturizer that feels comfortable.

EVERY DAY

Protect your skin

Use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. Reapply at least every two hours outdoors and after swimming or sweating, following the product label.

EVENING

Cleanse and moisturize

Gently remove sunscreen and makeup, then apply moisturizer. Keep the rest of your routine simple while your skin settles.

When to get professional advice

If your skin is severely painful, swollen, cracking, oozing, or not improving, speak with a healthcare professional or dermatologist. Persistent irritation may need more than a skincare routine change.
