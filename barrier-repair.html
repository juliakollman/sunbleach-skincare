Skin barrier, meet your match

Give your skin a little extra love.

Dry desert air, hot sunny days, and constant air conditioning can leave skin feeling tight and uncomfortable. Learn what your skin barrier does and how to build a simple routine that supports it.

Build your routine

Let's start with the basics

What is your skin barrier?

Your skin barrier is the outermost layer of your skin. It helps keep moisture in and protect your skin from irritants and things in the environment.

When the barrier is struggling, skin may feel dry, rough, tight, flaky, or more sensitive than usual. Those signs can have different causes, so pay attention to what your skin is telling you.

The goal: keep your routine gentle, support hydration, and avoid doing too much at once.

Desert skin reality

Why can dry climates be tough on skin?

Low humidity can contribute to moisture loss, while hot weather and air conditioning can leave some people feeling extra dry.

A gentle cleanser, a moisturizer that suits your skin, and daily sun protection are useful starting points for a straightforward routine.

Know what your skin needs

Signs your skin may need a reset

01 · TIGHTNESS

That tight feeling

If your skin feels tight after washing or throughout the day, your cleanser or routine may be too drying for you.

02 · FLAKINESS

Dry or flaky patches

Roughness and flaking can be signs of dryness. A moisturizer and a gentler routine may help support comfortable skin.

03 · SENSITIVITY

More easily irritated

If products that usually feel fine suddenly sting, consider simplifying your routine and pausing products that seem irritating.

These signs don't prove that your skin barrier is damaged. If irritation is persistent, severe, or getting worse, consider talking with a healthcare professional or dermatologist.

Keep your routine simple

Less can be more.

When skin feels dry or sensitive, adding lots of new products can make it harder to figure out what works. Start with a few basics and introduce changes gradually.

You don't need a complicated 10-step routine to take care of your skin.

Three helpful habits

Be gentle. Use a mild cleanser and avoid scrubbing skin aggressively.

Moisturize consistently. Choose a moisturizer that feels comfortable and helps with your dryness.

Protect your skin. Wear broad-spectrum sunscreen during the day and follow the product's directions.

Your everyday reset

A simple barrier-support routine

Morning

  1. Cleanse gently.
    Use a gentle cleanser if needed. If your skin feels very dry, avoid over-cleansing.
  2. Moisturize.
    Apply a moisturizer that suits your skin and helps it feel comfortable.
  3. Apply sunscreen.
    Use broad-spectrum sunscreen as directed and reapply according to its label.

Evening

  1. Wash away the day.
    Use a gentle cleanser to remove sunscreen, makeup, and everyday buildup.
  2. Moisturize again.
    Apply your moisturizer while following the product's directions.
  3. Give your skin time.
    Keep the routine consistent and avoid adding several new products at once.

Ingredient spotlight

Ingredients worth knowing

HUMECTANTS

Glycerin

Glycerin is a humectant, which means it helps attract water to the outer layer of the skin. It is commonly used in moisturizing products.

EMOLLIENTS

Squalane

Squalane is an emollient used to help soften skin and improve the feel of a formula. It can be found in moisturizers and facial care products.

BARRIER LIPIDS

Ceramides

Ceramides are lipids naturally found in the skin barrier. Skincare products containing ceramides are designed to support the skin's moisture barrier.

Ingredients work differently depending on the full formula and the person using it. Choose products that fit your skin, and patch test new products when appropriate.

Common questions

Barrier repair FAQs

How long does it take to improve a skin barrier?

It depends on what is causing the irritation or dryness. A gentle routine may help, but there is no single timeline that works for everyone. Persistent symptoms may need professional advice.

Should I stop using exfoliating products?

If your skin is irritated, stinging, or unusually dry, consider pausing exfoliating products until your skin feels comfortable again. If symptoms continue, seek advice from a dermatologist.

Can I still use active ingredients?

It depends on your skin and the product. If your skin is feeling sensitive, keeping things simple can help you avoid additional irritation. Reintroduce products gradually if appropriate.

Is moisturizer enough to protect my skin?

Moisturizer helps with hydration, but it doesn't replace sunscreen or other sun-protective habits. A simple routine usually includes gentle cleansing, moisturizing, and daytime sun protection.

Keep it comfortable

Your skin doesn't need a complicated routine.

Start with the basics, pay attention to how your skin responds, and give your routine time. Small, consistent habits can make skincare feel much easier.
