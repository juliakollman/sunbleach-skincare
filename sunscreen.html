The sunscreen lowdown

What is mineral sunscreen?

Mineral sunscreen uses active ingredients such as zinc oxide or titanium dioxide to help protect skin from ultraviolet rays. It is also called physical sunscreen.

For desert living, daily sun protection matters because outdoor exposure can add up during walks, errands, workouts, and time spent outside.

Keep it simple: choose broad-spectrum protection, apply enough, and reapply as directed on the product label.

Let's talk white cast

Why can mineral sunscreen look white?

Mineral UV filters can leave a visible cast depending on the formula, amount applied, and skin tone. Some formulas use tint to help the product blend in more naturally.

A tinted formula may be an option if you prefer a more even-looking finish, but shade and texture preferences vary.

What to look for

Your sunscreen checklist

Your everyday routine

How to apply sunscreen

  1. Apply generously.
    Use sunscreen on all exposed skin, following the amount and directions on the product label.
  2. Make it the last skincare step.
    Apply sunscreen after moisturizer and before makeup, unless the product directions say otherwise.
  3. Don't forget easy-to-miss areas.
    Remember your ears, neck, hands, and the tops of your feet when exposed.
  4. Reapply regularly.
    Reapply at least every two hours outdoors, and after swimming, sweating, or towel drying, following label directions.
  5. Add extra sun protection.
    Use shade, sunglasses, protective clothing, and a hat. Sunscreen is one part of a sun-safe routine.

Good to know

Sunscreen FAQs

Do I need sunscreen on cloudy days?

Yes. Ultraviolet rays can reach your skin even when the sky is cloudy, so daily protection is still important when you are outside.

Is mineral sunscreen always better for sensitive skin?

Not necessarily. Skin can react differently to different formulas and ingredients. Check the ingredient list and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Can I rely on makeup with SPF?

SPF makeup can contribute to your routine, but people often apply less makeup than the amount needed for the labeled protection. Use a dedicated sunscreen and follow its directions.

Does tinted sunscreen mean I can apply less?

No. Apply the amount directed on the label and reapply as recommended. Tint does not replace proper application or reapplication.
