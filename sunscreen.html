The sunscreen download

Protection is always in.

Sunscreen is one important part of protecting your skin from ultraviolet (UV) radiation. Look for broad-spectrum protection and SPF 30 or higher, and follow the product's directions.

🛡️

Broad-spectrum matters

Broad-spectrum sunscreen protects against both UVA and UVB rays. SPF mainly describes protection against sunburn-causing UVB rays.

🪨

Meet mineral filters

Mineral sunscreens use zinc oxide, titanium dioxide, or both as active ingredients to help protect skin from UV radiation.

🔁

Reapply, please

Apply generously and reapply at least every two hours outdoors, and after swimming, sweating, or towel drying, as directed.

The white-cast question

Why does mineral SPF look white?

Some mineral sunscreen formulas can leave a pale or ashy-looking film, especially on deeper skin tones. The finish depends on the formula, the amount applied, and your skin tone.

  • Look for products that specifically describe their finish.
  • If you prefer tint, explore tinted sunscreen options made for your skin tone.
  • Test a product on your face or jawline when possible.
  • Choose a sunscreen you can apply generously and wear consistently.
Real talk: A product's marketing description does not guarantee that it will leave no white cast on every skin tone.

Your daily SPF ritual

Make it a habit, not a maybe.

  1. Start with your morning skin care.

    Cleanse and moisturize as needed before applying sunscreen.

  2. Apply enough.

    Follow the label directions and apply sunscreen generously to exposed skin.

  3. Don't forget easy-to-miss spots.

    Pay attention to your ears, neck, hairline, and the backs of your hands.

  4. Bring it with you.

    Reapply as directed and add shade, protective clothing, and a hat when possible.

Frequently asked questions

SPF, decoded.

Is mineral sunscreen always better for sensitive skin?

Not always. Some people prefer mineral formulas, but individual reactions depend on the full formula and your skin. If you have sensitive skin, consider fragrance-free options and patch-test new products when appropriate.

Can I skip sunscreen on cloudy days?

No. UV rays can reach your skin even when it is cloudy. Make sun protection part of your routine whenever you are exposed to daylight.

Does makeup with SPF replace sunscreen?

Makeup with SPF can add protection, but many people apply too little makeup to get the labeled level of protection. Use a dedicated sunscreen as directed, then apply makeup if you want.

Keep the routine balanced

Sun protection + skin comfort.

Sun care is one part of the bigger picture. If dry weather leaves your skin feeling tight or irritated, a simple, gentle routine can help support your skin barrier.
