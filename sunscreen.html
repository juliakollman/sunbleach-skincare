Know your SPF

What makes mineral sunscreen different?

Mineral sunscreens use zinc oxide, titanium dioxide, or both as active UV filters. They help protect skin by absorbing and scattering ultraviolet radiation. The best sunscreen is one you enjoy wearing and will apply consistently.

Why does white cast happen?

Zinc oxide and titanium dioxide are mineral particles that can appear white on skin. The visible effect depends on the formula, particle properties, amount applied, and your skin tone.

Some tinted formulas use iron oxides to reduce the visible pale finish. Tinted products may also offer some protection against visible light, depending on the formula and testing.

What to look for

  • Broad-spectrum protection: choose SPF 30 or higher for everyday use.
  • A suitable finish: look for clear, sheer, or tinted formulas that suit your preferences and skin tone.
  • Comfort: choose a texture you can apply generously without wanting to skip it.
  • Water resistance: if swimming or sweating, choose a water-resistant formula and follow its reapplication directions.

Make every application count

How to wear mineral SPF.

Application matters as much as formula. Using too little sunscreen can reduce the protection you receive.

STEP 01

Start with skincare

Apply your moisturizer first and let it settle. If you use makeup, sunscreen should go on before makeup.

STEP 02

Apply generously

Follow the label for the amount to use. Spread sunscreen evenly across exposed skin, including your ears, neck, and other commonly missed areas.

STEP 03

Reapply regularly

Reapply at least every two hours outdoors and after swimming or sweating, following the product's directions. Use shade, clothing, and a hat for extra protection.

Remember: cloudy days count, too.

Ultraviolet radiation can reach your skin even when the sky is overcast. Make sun protection part of your routine whenever you're spending time outside.
