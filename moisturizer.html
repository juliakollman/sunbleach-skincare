The summer skin edit

Hydration that gets the assignment.

A good summer moisturizer should feel comfortable on your skin and fit the weather, your skin type, and the rest of your routine. You do not need the heaviest cream on the shelf to care for dry skin.

💦

Look for humectants

Ingredients such as glycerin and hyaluronic acid help attract and hold water in the outer layers of skin.

🧴

Keep moisture in

Emollients and occlusive ingredients can help soften skin and reduce moisture loss. The best texture depends on your needs.

🌞

Match the moment

A lighter lotion may feel good during the day, while a richer cream can be useful when skin feels extra dry at night.

Your routine, simplified

Three steps to softer-feeling skin.

  1. Cleanse gently. Use a mild cleanser and avoid water that feels very hot.
  2. Moisturize. Apply your moisturizer while skin is slightly damp to help hold on to hydration.
  3. Protect. Finish your daytime routine with broad-spectrum sunscreen of SPF 30 or higher.

Frequently asked questions

Moisture myths, meet reality.

Should I moisturize if my skin gets oily in summer?

Usually, yes. Oily skin can still become dehydrated. Try a lightweight, non-comedogenic moisturizer and adjust the texture to what feels comfortable for you.

Can I skip moisturizer when it is humid?

Not necessarily. Your skin type and how it feels matter more than humidity alone. If your skin feels tight or dry, a suitable moisturizer may still help.

What if a moisturizer stings or makes my skin itchy?

Stop using it if irritation develops. Consider fragrance-free products, and seek medical advice if irritation is severe, persistent, or accompanied by swelling or a rash.
