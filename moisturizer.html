Start with your skin

Summer hydration, without the guesswork.

A good summer moisturizer should match your skin's needs, not just the temperature outside. Look for ingredients that attract water, help soften the skin, and support its natural protective barrier.

Why skin gets dry in summer

Warm weather can increase sweat and oil, but that doesn't automatically mean your skin is well hydrated. Low humidity can encourage water loss, while air conditioning may make indoor air feel especially dry.

Sun exposure, frequent washing, and using too many exfoliating products can also leave skin feeling tight or sensitive.

What to look for in a moisturizer

  • Humectants: ingredients such as glycerin that help attract and hold water in the outer layers of skin.
  • Emollients: ingredients that soften rough-feeling skin and improve its feel.
  • Occlusives: ingredients that help slow water loss by forming a protective layer.
  • A comfortable texture: a lighter lotion may suit oily skin, while drier skin may prefer a richer cream.

Your daily ritual

A simple summer routine.

Start with the basics and adjust based on how your skin feels. Consistency matters more than having a long shelf of products.

STEP 01

Cleanse gently

Use a gentle cleanser and lukewarm water. Avoid scrubbing or washing more often than your skin needs.

STEP 02

Moisturize

Apply moisturizer after cleansing, while your skin is still slightly damp, to help retain moisture.

STEP 03

Protect from the sun

Finish your morning routine with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. Reapply at least every two hours outdoors, and after swimming or sweating, following the product label.

Quick desert-skin tip

If your skin feels tight after cleansing, try a gentler cleanser or a richer moisturizer before adding more active ingredients. If dryness persists, burns, or becomes painful, consider speaking with a dermatologist.
